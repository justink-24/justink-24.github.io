ABOUT

I'm a CS student who likes coding projects that sit at the intersection of creativity and technology, places where a fun idea and a real technical problem overlap. That's part of why a game like 2048 was worth building: it's small enough to finish, but there's still genuine logic and design underneath it.

I'm also drawn to machine learning and to understanding how artificial intelligence actually works under the hood, not just using the tools but digging into what's happening at the core of them. I'm still early in that journey, and this site is partly a record of what I build along the way.
